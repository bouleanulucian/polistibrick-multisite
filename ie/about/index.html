We build today the homes of tomorrow.

Polistibrick is the ICF (Insulated Concrete Forms) system European patented system that has redefined what a premium home means: passive from the outset, no heating or cooling bills, built in weeks rather than years.

A simple vision: a house for life.

The traditional home was designed 200 years ago. It runs on bills: gas, firewood, electricity, air conditioning — costly, endless maintenance. This home is no longer an investment; it is an obligation.

Polistibrick is the modern alternative. A system that produces a home that no longer needs to consume energy to function. Walls with integrated insulation, floors with thermal lag, Passivhaus roofs delivered from the factory. Three prefabricated elements, one complete home.

We are the only ICF system in Europe that produces to order, at no extra cost. The only one with 3 predefined models plus full customisation. And we are the first to believe that a premium passive house should cost the same as a conventional home — not 35% more.
