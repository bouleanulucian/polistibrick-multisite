About the project

ROI accelerated through speed.

The developer acquired the land in March 2023, aiming to deliver 24 passive houses in 2024.

They evaluated 3 systems: brick + ETICS, timber home (CLT), Polistibrick. Polistibrick won on 3 axes: lead time (6 months vs 14 months for brick), cost (only 5% above brick, but with A+++ performance), predictability (prefabricated panels, no surprises on site).

Construction started in July 2023. The 24 units were grouped into 4 modules of 6 homes each. The permanent 12-person team worked simultaneously on 2 modules.

Final handover: January 2024. Sales: 24 units in 28 days, all at +18% above sector average.

Construction timeline

From foundation to key handover.

  1. Month 1

    Foundations + supporting slabs

    Excavation and foundations for all 24 units. Local crew, 4 weeks.

  2. Month 2-3

    Modules 1+2: walls + floors + roof

    12 structural homes assembled in 8 weeks. Team of 12 split across 2 homes simultaneously.

  3. Month 3-4

    Modules 3+4: walls + floors + roof

    The remaining 12 homes are assembled. In parallel, modules 1+2 enter the finishing phase.

  4. Month 4-5

    Building services + joinery + finishes

    Ventilation, electricity, plumbing for all units. Passivhaus-certified joinery.

  5. Month 6

    PHPP verification + handover

    Blower Door test on every unit. All achieve Passivhaus certification on the first attempt.
