About the project

How it was

The family bought land in Cluj-Napoca in 2023 with the intention of building a forever home. They consulted 4 architects and 3 builders. Two proposed brick, one AAC blocks, one Polistibrick.

After running the numbers, they found that the cost difference between a traditional passive house (brick + additional insulation) and Polistibrick was nearly 30%. Plus, a build time of 5 weeks vs 5 months.

Construction took place between April and May 2024. The partner builder's 3-person team installed MBK 270 walls in 9 days, PBK floors in 2 days, TBK roof in 3 days. Concrete pour: one day. Finishes and services: 4 weeks.

The family moved in July 2024.

Construction timeline

From foundation to keys in the door.

  1. Week 1

    Foundation + slab support

    Excavation, continuous foundation, ground-floor slab. Local crew, 4 days.

  2. Weeks 2-3

    Installation MBK + PBK + TBK

    The Polistibrick partner team (3 people) assembles wall, floor and roof formwork. Like LEGO.

  3. Week 4

    Concrete pour + joinery

    One trade for everything. Installation of triple-glazed windows and exterior doors.

  4. Week 5

    Building services + couverture

    Electrics, plumbing, MVHR. Ceramic tiles on the roof.

  5. Month 2-3

    Interior and exterior finishes

    Plaster, paint, parquet, kitchen furniture. Silicate render on the facade.

  6. Month 4

    Move-in + final checks

    Blower Door test (n50 = 0.48 — below Passivhaus limit). Acceptance and move-in.
