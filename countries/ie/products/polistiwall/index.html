Why do homes lose heat?

In conventional masonry, heat escapes at the ponts thermiques. Polistiwall isole on the outside only : continuous 20 or 25 cm EPS. Inside: reinforced concrete, without a panel. No break in the façade insulation.

Questions sur le produit

Wall 200/250, PBK, TBK — composition and spans.

What is the difference from Polistibrick?

Polistiwall n’a no inner panel (no fibre cement or inner EPS). The inside is reinforced concrete. EPS insulation is fitted only on the outside (20 or 25 cm). See also Polistibrick.

Which model should you choose: Wall 200 or Wall 250?

Wall 200: 20 cm EPS, U = 0.15, R = 6.68, phase shift 7.8 h. Wall 250: 25 cm EPS, U = 0.12, R = 8.30, phase shift 8.5 h. Ubakus data sheets. Concrete 15–25 cm.

What is the wall made of?

Inside → outside: 15–25 cm reinforced concrete (exposed face), graphite EPS insulation λ = 0.031 (20 or 25 cm). No inner panel. Details in the tab Wall.

What span for PBK floors?

PBK 200 up to 6 m, PBK 250 up to 7.5 m, PBK 300 up to 9 m — or a conventional floor. See the tab Floor.

Is the TBK roof compulsory?

No. Panel TBK / SIP250 (maximum span 4.5 m between supports) or a conventional roof.

The Polistiwall assembly, from A to Z.

Inner formwork, floor, reinforcement, exterior insulation and pouring. Then the formwork is removed, leaving the concrete ready to finish. 55 seconds.

  1. 1Corner pieces and plywood: the perimeter is closed
  2. 2Floor, then a metal sheet in each opening
  3. 3Reinforcement of walls and floor slab
  4. 4EPS panels: the formwork closes up
  5. 5Pouring, then striking the formwork. The concrete remains exposed
