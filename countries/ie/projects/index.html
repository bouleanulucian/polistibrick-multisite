Riva is a long home on a single level, designed for a standard plot. No stairs, no upstairs to maintain — everything you use daily is on one level, including at 70.

The living space is at the centre: living room, kitchen and dining room in a single 48 m² volume, opening onto the covered terrace through a large sliding door. The kitchen has an island and a window onto the garden.

Bedrooms are grouped at the other end, separated from the living space by a corridor. The main bedroom, 14 m², has direct access to the terrace. The bathroom and separate WC serve all three bedrooms without passing through the living room.
