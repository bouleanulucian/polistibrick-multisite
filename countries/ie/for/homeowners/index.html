The real questions that you ask yourself.

EPS (expanded polystyrene) is 90% air by volume and 10% polystyrene. It is 100 % recyclable at end of life and emits no harmful gases. Over the full life cycle (50 years), it has a lower carbon footprint than timber frame because it avoids decades of heating. Our LCA report is available on request.

No. RE2020 sets carbon targets which Polistibrick far exceeds thanks to its exceptional energy performance. A typical simulation for your project is available. The rumour that "concrete is banned" is false — RE2020 simply penalises standard uninsulated concrete.

The house reaches 80% completion in 4 to 6 weeks for a 150–200 m² house. Overall, you can move in within 5-7 months (depending on finishes and permit). 2–3 times faster than conventional construction.

Polistibrick is a conventional reinforced concrete structure. Amendments (opening a wall, extending) are done with the same tools and techniques as on a traditional home. Your architect or a local contractor can intervene. No dependency on Polistibrick after construction.

Yes. Polistibrick is the structural core of your home — you choose the exterior finish (render, stone, brick slips, timber cladding, etc.) and the interior finish (plasterboard, plaster, paint). You will never see the concrete or EPS. The house looks exactly as you want it: Provençal, contemporary or traditional.

A passive house is eligible for eco-PTZ up to €50,000 (zero-interest loan, 15 years). Several banks also offer green loans at preferential rates. Your bank adviser takes energy savings into account to increase your borrowing capacity.
