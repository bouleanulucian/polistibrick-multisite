For builders and professionals

Combien d'ouvriers faut-il pour monter une maison ?

A single team of 3 personnes builds a 100 m² single-storey home in less than 3 weeks, nearly complete. Where conventional construction mobilises up to 5 different crews (masons, external insulation, internal insulation, drywallers, airtightness specialists), Polistibrick requires only one.

Polistibrick remplace combien de corps de métier ?

Five, in a single installation : masonry, external insulation (ETICS), internal insulation, cladding/finishing and airtightness. One product and one team — instead of five materials and five different crews.

La plaque de finition, est-ce du placo ?

Non : c'est du fibre-ciment. In a single product, it withstands fire, water and noise — where a conventional home would require different panels for each of these properties.

How do electrics and plumbing run through the structure?

Services run through the formwork before it is closed, exactly like in a plasterboard partition. Ducts are therefore integrated during installation, not handled as a separate package.

Does the system help meet RE2020?

Oui. L'Airtightness is ensured by the system itself, and insulation is continuous, no thermal bridges — two key points of RE2020. Airtightness tests pass on the first attempt.

Is it earthquake-resistant? Does it allow large cantilevers?

Oui. La structure est un concrete armé monolithique, antisismique. Elle autorise aussi de large cantilevers — ideal for contemporary architecture (flat roofs, cantilevered volumes).

About the system

What exactly is Polistibrick?

Polistibrick is a patented European ICF (Insulated Concrete Forms) system. Specifically, prefabricated formwork made of expanded polystyrene (EPS) that assembles like LEGO and is filled with reinforced concrete. The result is a monolithic, thermally and acoustically insulated wall, ready for finishes.

What is the difference from a classic ICF system?

3 éléments : (1) we offer 3 predefined models plus custom production at no charge; (2) we integrate pre-positioned reinforcement — it doesn't need to be tied on site; (3) we hold a European patent, so it is a unique system.

Isn't EPS dangerous? Doesn't it burn?

Raw EPS is combustible — but the insulation we use is ignifugé (self-extinguishing). And above all, in the Polistibrick system it is protected by fibre cement boards, classées A1 (incombustible), the highest fire reaction class. Result: the system does not burn — the insulator stays enclosed between the reinforced concrete at the wall core and fibre-cement at the surface, never exposed to flames.

Do the walls breathe? Will there be damp?

« Breathing » is above all a popular expression : a wall that keeps air out. Specifically, Polistibrick walls manage water vapour in a controlled way (μ = 30-70), enough to prevent condensation without losing heat. EPS does not absorb water (closed cells) — so no humidity or mould issues, and an estimated lifespan of over 50 years. Please note: installing a MVHR (mechanical ventilation with heat recovery) is mandatory, regardless of the building system chosen.

About implementation

How long does construction with Polistibrick take?

Comptez environ 4 weeks per floor for a 150–200 m² ground-floor house (structure). Panels, spanning the full storey height, are installed like classic metal formwork. It is much faster than traditional construction: everything is done in a single assembly, without the usual waiting times (render drying, façade insulation, cladding).

Do I need a specialist builder?

Non. N'importe quelle entreprise de construction peut monter le système : Polistibrick forme les équipes de l'entreprise directement sur le chantier, during construction, then comes inspect the work at the end. Les entreprises qui réalisent plusieurs maisons avec le système rejoignent le réseau de partenaires agréés Polistibrick. Learn more.

Is Polistibrick compatible with traditional architecture?

Yes, our system accepts any type of exterior finish — render, stone, wood, facing brick, ceramic panels. Same for interiors. Polistibrick is the invisible structure; the design is yours.

Warranty and service

What warranty do you offer?

Il faut distinguer deux niveaux. Site guarantees — the perfect completion guarantee (1 year), biennial (2 years) and especially decennial (10 years) for the structure — supplied by the company or builder installing the system. La garantie produit Polistibrick, as manubillr, covers compliance with marking CE and declared performance (strength, insulation, fire reaction). The materials — fibre-cement, graphite EPS and reinforced concrete — are designed to last over 50 years.

Is a Polistibrick home easy to insure?

Yes, without any difficulty. Structurally, Polistibrick is identical to classic reinforced concrete wall technology : the load-bearing wall is cast-in-place reinforced concrete, a current and fully recognised technique. The home is therefore insured comme n'importe quelle construction en concrete armé — construction insurance and ten-year warranty included — at n'importe quel assureur.
