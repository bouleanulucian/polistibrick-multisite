The roots

Everything starts sur le chantier.

I come from a family of masons. I set foot in this world at eight. By twelve, I was already spending summers on building sites, alongside my grandfather, Valică Bouleanu — who paid me like a skilled worker.

Throughout my career, I have built with every system: timber to stone, ICF included. I always knew ICF was the way forward. But it only solves one problem — insulation. And nobody wants to pay more for nothing extra.

Le constat

So I studied every defect.

I identified, one by one, the limits of every ICF system on the market.

Ils se fissurent

Some formwork fails under concrete pressure at the moment of pouring.

Impossible to reinforce

Others do not accept reinforcing steel — a major structural limitation.

Limited-height for

Others cannot handle pours at height and require multiple pours.

Heavy to install

Even the most robust ones are slow and cumbersome to assemble on site.

And all of them, without exception, offer insulation only.

The answer

Six years. Six versions. One single system.

Today, I can tell you one thing: Polistibrick brings together every stage of passive construction. That is where its name comes from — 5 in 1 — where ICF systems are only a 2 in 1 : masonry and insulation.

Polistibrick was born from others' mistakes. And I commit to reinvesting in research every year, as I always have.
