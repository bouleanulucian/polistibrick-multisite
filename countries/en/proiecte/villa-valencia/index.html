About proiect

From idea to move-in in 4 months.

The family bought the land in 2022, intending to build a retirement home. They travelled across Europe to see passive houses in different climates. They tested ICF technologies in several countries.

Au ales Polistibrick din 3 motive: (1) the Valencia factory meant delivery in 3 days; (2) MBK 210 was optimised for temperate-warm climates; (3) the European patent gave them the assurance of a one-of-a-kind system, not a commodity product.

Construction started in March 2023. The joinery arrived a week late, but everything else went according to plan. Move-in: 1 May 2023.

After the first summer (15 May - 30 September 2023), the family opened the electricity meter app. Total electricity consumption: 290 kWh/month. Vecinul lor: 1.840 kWh/month.
