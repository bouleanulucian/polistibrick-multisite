About proiect

ROI accelerated through speed.

The developer acquired the land in March 2023, aiming to deliver 24 passive houses in 2024. Bugetul: 5,8 M €.

They evaluated 3 systems: brick + ETICS, timber house (CLT), Polistibrick. Polistibrick won on 3 fronts: timp (6 months vs 14 months for brick), cost (just 5% above brick, but with A+++ performance), predictibilitate (prefabricated panels, no surprises on site).

Construction started in July 2023. The 24 units were grouped into 4 modules of 6 houses each. A permanent team of 12 people worked on 2 modules simultaneously.

Final handover: January 2024. Sales: 24 units in 28 days, all at +18% above the area average.

Construction timeline

From foundation to key in the door.

    Foundations + supporting slabs

    Excavation and foundations for all 24 units. Local team 4 weeks.

  2. Luna 2-3

    Module 1+2: walls + floors + roof

    12 structural shells erected in 8 weeks. Team of 12 people split across 2 houses simultaneously.

  3. Luna 3-4

    Module 3+4: walls + floors + roof

    Restul 12 case ridicate. Meanwhile, modules 1+2 move into finishes.
