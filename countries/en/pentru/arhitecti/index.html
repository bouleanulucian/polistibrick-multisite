Architectural freedom

What you can do with Polistibrick.

  • Spans up to 9 m without trusses or visible beams (PBK + TBK)
  • Walls curbi achievable with MBK sectioned and assembled on site
  • Cantilever-uri up to 2.5 m with additional reinforcement
  • Ferestre full-height with special patented profiles
  • Zone vitrate mari no thermal bridges (thanks to continuous EPS)
  • Etajeri G+1, G+2, G+3 — system certified for tall residential buildings
  • Subsol pasiv with MBK adapted for sloping ground
  • Roof vegetal (green roof) compatible — TBK supports an extra 200 kg/m²
  • Roof plat or with any pitch, however shallow or steep
  • Geometrii custom manufactured on demand at no additional cost
  • Adjacent renovation — MBK connects to existing structures
  • Modulare — perfect for residential developments, social housing, hotels
