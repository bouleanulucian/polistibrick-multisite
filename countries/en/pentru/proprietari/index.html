Benefits for you

What you get concret.

Electricity bill under 50 €/month

Polistibrick reduces energy consumption by 70%. No more paying 300-500 €/month on gas in winter and air conditioning in summer.

Construction in 1 month

Your house is ready in 4-6 weeks vs 4-5 months with brick. Less stress, fewer delays, fewer surprises.

Quiet and safety

15 cm reinforced concrete walls + 20-25 cm EPS: acoustic insulation > 50 dB, earthquake resistance and A1 fire resistance.

Waterproof, no mould

The closed-cell EPS does not absorb water. You will never have problems with damp, mould or condensation in the walls.

A lifetime investment

50+ year warranty on the structure. The house's value rises in step with energy prices. A passive house sells for 15-25% more than a classic one.

Confort termic constant

Iarna 21°C, summer 24°C. The thermal inertia of the concrete + EPS maintains a natural temperature, without radiators or air conditioning.
