What the estimate does not include

What needs to be budgeted additionally for a complete house.

Foundations and earthworks

~€80–150/m² depending on terrain and groundwater level. Polistibrick does not manubill foundations — use a local builder.

Joinery (windows + doors)

For passive house: triple glazing, low-e, thermally broken frame. Average budget €350–600/m² of window (installation included).

Technical installations

Electrics, plumbing, heating, MVHR. Average budget €80–150/m² of home, depending on the technology level chosen.

Interior finishes

Render, paint, tiles, parquet, kitchen furniture. Average budget €200–500/m² of house, depending on standard chosen.

Façade (cladding)

Silicate render, stone, wood, ceramic panels. Polistibrick is the structure, you choose the look. Budget €30–150/m² façade.

Installation labour

Certified partner builder: ~€20–30/m² wall + floor (panel installation and concrete pour). Much less than for brick.
