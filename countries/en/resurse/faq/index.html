About sistem

What exactly is Polistibrick?

Polistibrick is a European-patented ICF (Insulated Concrete Forms) system. In practice, they are prefabricated formwork made from expanded polystyrene (EPS) that assembles like LEGO and is filled with reinforced concrete. The result is a monolithic wall, thermally and acoustically insulated, ready for finishes.

What is different compared with a conventional ICF system?

3 lucruri: (1) we offer 3 predefined models plus custom production at no charge; (2) we integrate pre-positioned reinforcement — it doesn't need to be tied on site; (3) we hold a European patent, so it is a one-of-a-kind system.

Isn't EPS dangerous? Doesn't it burn?

EPS on its own is combustible, but in the Polistibrick system it is ENCASED between 15 cm of reinforced concrete and fire-resistant renders. The overall system has class A1 (non-combustible) and withstands over 240 minutes of fire (REI 240) — the highest class possible.

Do the walls breathe? Won't there be moisture?

The walls breathe in a controlled way (μ = 30-70), enough to prevent condensation, but not so much that they lose heat. EPS does not absorb water (closed-cell), so you will have no moisture or mould issues. 50+ year warranty.

About cost

How does Polistibrick cost compare with brick?

Polistibrick costs just +5% above the price of a conventional house — for an A+++ passive house. Compared with a brick house upgraded to passive standard, Polistibrick is −30% cheaper.

How much will I save on bills over 25 years?

For a 150 m² house we estimate total savings of ~€43,500 over 25 years (gas + electricity + maintenance). In practice, many owners end up paying almost nothing for heating and cooling.

Are there any tax incentives or subsidies?

Yes, in many EU countries there are subsidies for passive houses (Casa Verde, KfW in Germany, MaPrimeRénov' in France, etc.). We help you with the application paperwork.

About execution

How long does construction with Polistibrick take?

For a single-family house of 150-200 m²: 4-6 weeks from foundation to finishes (rough). Vs 4-5 months with the conventional system. The difference comes from the rapid assembly (like LEGO) and the absence of waiting times (plaster drying, facade insulation, etc.).

Do I need a specialist builder?

We recommend a Polistibrick-certified builder (3-day course). The cost of the course is usually recovered from the savings on the first project. List of certified partners at contact.

Is Polistibrick compatible with traditional architecture?

Yes, our system allows any type of exterior finish — render, stone, timber, exposed brick, ceramic tiles. The same goes for the interior. Polistibrick is the hidden structure, the design is yours.

Warranty and service

What warranty do you offer?

50-year warranty on the structure, 25 years on the thermal properties, 10 years on the finishes (when applied by our certified partners).

Can a Polistibrick house be insured easily?

Da. The system is recognised by all major European insurers. Thanks to A1 fire resistance and structural solidity, insurance premiums can be lower than for a brick house.
