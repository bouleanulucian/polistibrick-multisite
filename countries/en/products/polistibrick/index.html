Why do homes lose heat?

In conventional masonry, heat escapes at the ponts thermiques — joints, connections, every break in the insulation. Polistibrick wraps the concrete : Inner EPS + Outer EPS, continuous MBK interlocking. No break at each joint.

Questions sur le produit

MBK, PBK, TBK — models, composition and spans.

Which MBK formwork should you choose : 210, 270 or 300 ?

MBK 210 (U = 0.14) for RE2020/temperate climates. MBK 270 (U = 0.11) for the standard passive level. MBK 300 (U = 0.10) for Passive Plus or cold climates. All three: two fibre cement faces, 120 × 300 cm format.

What is the wall formwork made of?

From inside to outside: 12 mm fibre cement, inner EPS, 12–25 cm reinforced concrete (poured on site), outer EPS, 8 mm fibre cement. Fire A1. Details in the tab Formwork.

What span for PBK floors?

PBK 200 up to 6 m, PBK 250 up to 7.5 m, PBK 300 up to 9 m. Floor blocks = 8 mm fibre cement + EPS; the reinforced concrete is poured after installation. See the tab Floor.

Is TBK mandatory for the roof?

No. You can install the panel TBK / SIP250 (U = 0.13, 25 cm) or a conventional roof. The same panel as SIP250.

What thickness of concrete is used in the MBK wall?

The structural concrete is sized between 12 and 25 cm depending on the project (Eurocode 2). The stated total thicknesses (38/44/47 cm) are based on 15 cm of concrete.
