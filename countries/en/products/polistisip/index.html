Why do houses lose heat?

Most lose energy through the ponts thermiques — where solid timber passes through the insulation. PolistiSIP corrects this weak point with the technology I-Joist STEICO : continuous thermal barrier, up to 40% greater thermal efficiency.

  • ✓Timber structure sandwich (SIP) — no concrete poured in the wall
  • ✓I-Joist STEICO — minimal contact between the panel skins
  • ✓SIP300 wall — 30 cm, U=0.11, thermal lag 11.3 h, 23 kg/m²
