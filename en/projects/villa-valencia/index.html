About the project

From idea to move-in in 4 months.

The family bought the land in 2022 with the intention of building a retirement home. They travelled across Europe to visit passive houses in different climates. They tested ICF technologies in several countries.

They chose Polistibrick for 3 reasons: (1) the Valencia plant enabled delivery in 3 days; (2) MBK 210 was optimised for temperate-warm climate; (3) the European patent gave them the assurance of a one-of-a-kind system, not a commodity product.

Construction started in March 2023. Joinery arrived a week late, but the rest proceeded according to plan. Move-in: 1 May 2023.

After the first summer (15 May - 30 September 2023), the family opened the electricity meter app. Total electricity consumption: 290 kWh/month. Their neighbour: 1,840 kWh/month.
