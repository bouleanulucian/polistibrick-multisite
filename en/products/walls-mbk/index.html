5 functions in a single product

One formwork. A whole house.

Classic brick needs more than 10 materials and 4-5 different crews for a finished wall. MBK replaces them all:

  • Formwork: the modular structure that receives the concrete
  • Thermal insulation: Continuous EPS, no thermal bridges, up to 30 cm thickness (MBK 300)
  • Acoustic insulation: premium sound reduction through the EPS + reinforced concrete combination
  • Load-bearing structure: 12-25 cm reinforced concrete — earthquake and A1 fire resistance
  • Finish substrate: flat wall, ready to plaster or paint directly

Why MBK

Unmatched advantages in the ICF category.

Premium acoustic insulation

The combination of high-density EPS + reinforced concrete delivers sound reduction of over 50 dB — guaranteed quiet in any environment.

Natural airtightness

Monolithic concrete poured in a single stage — no joints, no weak points. Passes the Blower Door test (n50 < 0.6) without additional membranes.

A1 fire resistance

The highest fire reaction class — non-combustible. A1 fibre-cement panels do not contribute to fire and slow flame propagation.

Waterproof from construction

Closed-cell airtight EPS does not absorb water. Immune to humidity, mould and degradation over time. 50+ year warranty.

3× faster construction

A 2-3 person team raises a 100 m² wall in a single day. Formwork assembles like LEGO, concrete is poured once.

Customisation at no cost

The only ICF system that manufactures special dimensions to order, with no price surcharge — a wall tailored precisely to your project.
