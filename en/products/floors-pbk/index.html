Spans of 9 m without beams.

PBK is the prefabricated floor system that supports standard spans up to 9 metres without visible beams. It combines polystyrene, fibre-cement and reinforced concrete for a thermal lag of 10,8 heures — cool in summer, warm in winter, no additional insulation.

Patented hybrid technology

Three materials, a single slab.

PBK combines into a single prefabricated panel what previously required 3 different teams to assemble on site:

  • polystyrene — the central insulating body, thermal lag of up to 10.8 hours
  • High-strength fibre-cement — the outer faces, A1 fire resistance + a substrate for finishes
  • Reinforced concrete structurel — poured into the internal ribs after installation, spans up to 9 m
  • Pre-assembled at the factory — arrives on site ready to position, not to assemble

Why PBK

Structural and thermal advantages.

Thermal lag 10.8 h

Heat from 14:00 reaches the interior only by 00:00, when it has already cooled outside. Air conditioning reduced to a minimum.

9 m spans without beams

Open space, free plan, no visible columns or beams inside. Maximum architectural freedom.

Prefabricated at the factory

A team of 3 people installs the floor of a house in a single day.

A1 fire resistance

The highest fire reaction class — non-combustible. A1 fibre-cement panels do not contribute to fire and slow flame propagation.

Seulement 65 kg/m²

3× lighter than a classic concrete slab. Simpler foundation, cheaper transport, safer handling.

Acoustic insulation between floors

The EPS + fibre cement combination absorbs footsteps and airborne noise between floors. Guaranteed quiet.
