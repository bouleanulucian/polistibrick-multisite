TBK panel structure

A finished roof, to your dimensions.

TBK combines high-density OSB on the exterior with graphite EPS polystyrene on the interior. The result: the most performant prefabricated passive roof on the market.

  • 18 mm waterproof OSB-3 on the outside — substrate for any type of covering (tile, sheet metal, shingle, green)
  • Graphite EPS 220 mm — λ = 0.031 W/mK, Passivhaus performance by design
  • Interior fibre-cement layer — fire resistance + substrate for finishes (paint, plaster, wood)
  • Single prefabricated panel — no thermal bridges, no gaps, no infiltration
Why TBK

The most efficient prefabricated passive roof.

Passivhaus performance

U=0.13 W/m²K — below the strict Passivhaus certification threshold (0.15). The home stays warm in winter from occupant and appliance heat alone.

No additional insulation required

All classic roof systems require +10-15 cm of mineral wool to become passive. TBK is ready from the factory — one panel, one product.

9 m spans without trusses

Self-supporting panels eliminate the need for columns, buttresses or visible trusses. Clean ceilings, open space under the roof.

Only 22 kg/m²

8× lighter than a classic concrete floor slab. Handled by 2 people, installed with a small crane. Simpler structure below.

Compatible with any covering

Waterproof OSB-3 accepts ceramic tiles, concrete, metal, bitumen shingles or green roof. Total design freedom.

Natural airtightness

Joints with patented Polistibrick special profiles. Passes the Blower Door test without adding external membranes.
