What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by the browser on your device. They allow the site to recognise you on repeat visits.

Cookies we use

(1) Essential — for the site to function. (2) Analytics — Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. (3) Marketing — Meta Pixel, Google Ads to show you relevant ads.

Controlul cookies

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies from the banner displayed on first visit. You can change your choice at any time via your browser settings or by contacting us directly.

Third-party cookies

Folosim servicii Google (Analytics, Maps), Meta (Pixel, Conversions API), YouTube (embed video). Each has its own privacy policy.
