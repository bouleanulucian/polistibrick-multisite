How the calculation works

Methodology transparent.

Construction cost

Brick: ~€1,000/m² at non-compliant standard. Polistibrick: ~€1,050/m² (+5%) for an A+++ passive house. Includes materials and labour, excluding interior or exterior finishes.

Energy cost

Based on typical climate consumption: brick averages 100–180 kWh/m²/year for heating and air conditioning. Polistibrick (passive) consumes 25–45 kWh/m²/year. Average reduction of 70%.

Maintenance over 25 years

Brick: thermal insulation retrofit at 15 years (~€5,000), monthsture-related repairs, façade renovation. Polistibrick: none of this — EPS does not absorb water, does not degrade, the monolithic structure does not crack.

Energy inflation

Over the last 14 years, electricity prices in the EU have risen by approximately 4% per year. Our calculation incorporates annual compounding — real savings over 25 years exceed today's difference × 25.
