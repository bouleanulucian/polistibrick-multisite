For builders and professionals

How many workers are needed to install a home?

A single team of 3 people builds a 100 m² single-storey home in less than 3 weeks, nearly complete. Where conventional construction mobilises up to 5 different crews (masons, external insulation, internal insulation, drywallers, airtightness specialists), Polistibrick requires only one.

How many trades does Polistibrick replace?

Five, in a single installation: masonry, external insulation (ETICS), internal insulation, cladding/finishing and airtightness. One product and one team — instead of five materials and five different crews.

Is the finish board plasterboard?

No: it is fibre-cement. In a single product, it withstands fire, water and noise — where a conventional home would require different panels for each of these properties.

How do electrics and plumbing run through the structure?

Services run through the formwork before it is closed, exactly like in a plasterboard partition. Ducts are therefore integrated during installation, not handled as a separate package.

Does the system help meet RE2020?

Yes. Airtightness is ensured by the system itself, and insulation is continuous, with no thermal bridges — two key points of RE2020. Airtightness tests pass on the first attempt.

Is it earthquake-resistant? Does it allow large cantilevers?

Yes. The structure is a monolithic reinforced concrete shell, seismic-resistant. It also allows large cantilevers — ideal for contemporary architecture (flat roofs, cantilevered volumes).

A short training is enough to take on the system. Become a partner to join the network, get trained and receive projects near you.

About the system

What exactly is Polistibrick?

Polistibrick is a patented European ICF (Insulated Concrete Forms) system. Specifically, prefabricated formwork made of expanded polystyrene (EPS) that assembles like LEGO and is filled with reinforced concrete. The result is a monolithic, thermally and acoustically insulated wall, ready for finishes.

What is the difference from a classic ICF system?

3 elements: (1) we offer 3 predefined models plus custom production at no charge; (2) we integrate pre-positioned reinforcement — it doesn't need to be tied on site; (3) we hold a European patent, so it is a one-of-a-kind system.

Isn't EPS dangerous? Doesn't it burn?

Raw EPS is combustible — but the insulation we use is fire-retardant (self-extinguishing). And above all, in the Polistibrick system it is protected by fibre cement boards, ranked A1 (incombustible), the highest fire reaction class. Result: the system does not burn — the insulator stays enclosed between the reinforced concrete at the wall core and fibre-cement at the surface, never exposed to flames.

Do the walls breathe? Will there be damp?

“Breathing” is above all a popular expression: a wall does not let air in. Specifically, Polistibrick walls manage water vapour in a controlled way (μ = 30-70), enough to prevent condensation without losing heat. EPS does not absorb water (closed cells) — so no humidity or mould issues, and an estimated lifespan of over 50 years. Please note: installing a MVHR (mechanical ventilation with heat recovery) is mandatory, regardless of the building system chosen.

About cost

How does Polistibrick cost compare with brick?

Polistibrick costs just +5% compared to the price of a conventional home — for an A+++ passive house. Compared to a brick home retrofitted to passive, Polistibrick is −30% more affordable.

How much will I save on bills over 25 years?

For a 150 m² home, we estimate total savings of approximately €43,500 over 25 years (gas + electricity + maintenance). In practice, many owners pay almost nothing for heating and air conditioning.

Are there tax incentives or subsidies?

Yes, in many EU countries there are subsidies for passive houses (Casa Verde in Romania, KfW in Germany, MaPrimeRénov' in France, etc.).

About implementation

How long does construction with Polistibrick take?

Allow approximately 4 weeks per floor for a 150–200 m² ground-floor house (structure). Panels, spanning the full storey height, are installed like classic metal formwork. It is much faster than traditional construction: everything is done in a single assembly, without the usual waiting times (render drying, façade insulation, cladding).

Do I need a specialist builder?

No. Any construction company can assemble the system: Polistibrick trains the contractor's crews directly on site, during construction, then inspects the work at the end. Companies that build several homes with the system join the Polistibrick approved partner network. Learn more.

Is Polistibrick compatible with traditional architecture?

Yes, our system accepts any type of exterior finish — render, stone, wood, facing brick, ceramic panels. Same for interiors. Polistibrick is the invisible structure; the design is yours.

Warranty and service

What warranty do you offer?

There are two levels to distinguish. Site guarantees — the perfect completion guarantee (1 year), biennial (2 years) and especially decennial (10 years) for the structure — supplied by the company or builder installing the system. The Polistibrick product warranty, as manufacturer, covers compliance with CE marking and declared performance (strength, insulation, fire reaction). The materials — fibre-cement, graphite EPS and reinforced concrete — are designed to last over 50 years.

Is a Polistibrick home easy to insure?

Yes, without any difficulty. Structurally, Polistibrick is identical to classic reinforced concrete wall technology: the load-bearing wall is cast-in-place reinforced concrete, a current and fully recognised technique. The home is therefore insured like any reinforced concrete construction — construction insurance and ten-year warranty included — with any insurer.
